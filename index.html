Who we are

Christian Academics Switzerland brings together believers who study and work in Swiss universities and research institutes. We are part of Good News for the University, an IFES Europe initiative that serves Christian scholars across the region.

Our local groups in Zurich and Lausanne exist so that Christians in academia can encourage one another: pursuing excellent, faithful scholarship under Christ’s lordship, and helping one another see how our fields point to God’s glory — and share that good news with others.

Whether you are new to Switzerland or have been here for years, you are welcome.

Local groups

We currently meet in two cities. Each group has its own rhythm; details may change each semester — check the calendar or contact a coordinator for the latest.

Zurich

ETH Zurich · University of Zurich · surrounding institutions

Our Zurich group gathers for Bible study, prayer, and occasional talks on faith and scholarship. Meetings are usually held on or near campus.

Coordinator
zurich@christianacademics.ch

Lausanne

EPFL · University of Lausanne · surrounding institutions

The Lausanne group welcomes Francophone and Anglophone members. We meet monthly to share a meal and to hear a presentation from one of our members about the intersection of their research and the gospel. We also host an English-language Bible study at EPFL and run the Ethics@EPFL lecture series.
